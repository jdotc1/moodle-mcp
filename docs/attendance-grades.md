# Attendance and grade math

Exact arithmetic (`Fraction`, `Decimal`); no floats, no `eval`.

**Attendance** — for whole, homogeneous units: required presence = ceil(min_frequency × total), absence limit = total − required. With weights/durations the computation is done in the real unit (class-hours) first. Example (tested): 72 h, 75 %, 10 h already absent → required 54, limit 18, margin 8 h = 4 meetings *only if* each meeting is 2 h. 30 h present → 24 h still needed (compare with the hours left, not with the margin).
Reported separately: registered absences, **pending roll calls** (past sessions without a record), last update, frequency so far, projection under explicit hypotheses, margin (also if all pending turn out absent), hours still needed, max skippable meetings (guaranteed vs optimistic), *critical* meetings (skipping one alone breaks the minimum), "already impossible", and per-date scenarios. Late / excused policies are arguments. The minimum % and the course total are **required inputs with a source** — Moodle's Attendance *points*, completion state and official presence are different things. Unknown total ⇒ provisional limits + warning. Exams and mandatory sessions are only known if you list them.

**Grades** — `scheme` = items (score or `null` for "not released", max, weight) + `formula` (`weighted` or an expression with `avg`, `sum`, `min`, `max`, `drop_lowest(n, …)`, `round_to(x, step)`, comparisons, `a if cond else b`) + optional `rounding` (step 0.5, `half_up|half_even|down|up|floor`), `pass_mark`, `source`. Missing items give worst/best bounds (never silent zeros); `required_score` solves by bisection (monotonic check) and returns `impossible`, `already_met` or `underdetermined`. The model may *interpret* the syllabus into a scheme; the tool keeps formula, source and assumptions in the result. Recovery rules: encode them in the formula (see tests) and cite the regulation and its date yourself — none is built in.
